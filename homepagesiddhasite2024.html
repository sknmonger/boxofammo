Daily Contemplation on Thirumantiram

The Mooladhara is the source of prana to manifest in anahatta the preserver Visnu. It takes the shakti energy towards the limbs of action that manifests life.

Sakti in Sadasiva manifested as World In the World His Sakti He manifested first, In the World as His Sakti’s Form He pervaded In the World His Sakti’s Powers He filled But He who this World’s’ creation conceived Was Sadasiva (the Linga). Sadasiva, who as Linga originally conceived the world’s creation, manifested in the world as His Sakti, pervaded there as His Sakti’s form, and filled it with His Sakti’s powers.

Bhairava Tantra
