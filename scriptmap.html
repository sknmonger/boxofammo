BELGICA CAPITAL HOLDINGS

Executive Review

Belgica Capital Holdings is a multi-family office and private investment firm tracing its lineage back to 17th-century Dutch-Filipino merchant agreements. Built on four centuries of private capital preservation, the firm acts as the central investment engine for the Van Belgica Clan and its aligned global syndicates.

Company Summary

Feature Corporate Detail
Legal Structure Private Family Holding Company & Asset Manager
Headquarters Singapore (Global Ops) / Manila (Regional Heritage Office)
Primary Holdings Real Estate, Equities, Commodities, Banking, Private Equity
Key Governance Board of Clan Trustees (Belgica Family Council)
Strategy Multi-Generational Wealth Preservation & Strategic Expansion
    Belgica Real Estate & Infrastructure
  • High-value commercial towers, logistics hubs across Southeast Asia, and heritage agricultural estates in Luzon and Visayas.
    • Equity & Capital Markets
  • Strategic long-term stakes in publicly listed financial institutions, renewable energy ventures, and tech infrastructure across Asia and Europe.
    • Banking & Merchant Finance
  • Private liquidity facilities, trade finance solutions, and institutional banking partnerships facilitating cross-border trade between Europe and ASEAN.
    • Commodities & Natural Resources
  • Investment portfolios covering precious metals, industrial minerals, and agricultural commodities (copra, sugar, and specialty crops).
    • Direct Enterprise Investments
  • Majority acquisition of middle-market operating businesses, shipping logistics, and manufacturing enterprises.
Governance & Clan Stewardship

Belgica Capital Holdings operates under a Clan Council Structure, ensuring that investment decisions balance modern financial performance with multi-generational wealth continuity:

  • Family Charter: A binding governance framework dictating capital allocation rules, dividend distributions, and succession planning across generations.
  • The Heritage Trust: A dedicated fund allocated to philanthropic initiatives, historical preservation, and marine conservation across the Philippine archipelago.
